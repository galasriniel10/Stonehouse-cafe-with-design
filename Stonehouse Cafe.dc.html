Our House

Stone walls, green tin roof, red door.

Doug and Christine O'Brien run the kitchen and the counter out of the old stone house on West Main, the one with the Japanese maple out front that goes crimson every October. Everything comes out of one small kitchen: eggs cracked to order, pork shoulder braised for carnitas, pickles put up in the back.

Come for a bottomless cup and the paper. Stay for the burger. Bring the whole family on a Saturday morning and we'll pull the tables together.
